Year 12 ATAR Applied Information Technology
Project Management – W3C Compliant Client Website

Deployed at https://rileytruran.github.io/PLAYTECH/

Type of Assessment

Project: 
Students research information technology-based ideas and processes to create digital solutions. This involves the application of project management approaches/techniques to a design process. The project can require students to refer to stimulus material. Questions can require students to refer to stimulus material. Stimulus material can include: extracts from newspapers or journal articles; screen captures of online media; multimedia and/or graphics; and/or a scenario.

Task Outline:
You will be working with a fictitious client for this task, represented by your teacher. This process requires the submission of design documentation to present to the client. For the purpose of this task, your team will assume the persona of a web development business.
Your ‘client’ will be chosen by you, with the end goal of marketing a product of your choice.
You have eight weeks to complete your overall project. Within that time, you will develop a Design Document which outlines all considerations for your client’s product and their target audience, and the final product – a fully completed website using HTML & CSS, due at the conclusion of the assessment
